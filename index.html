Encrypted backups for AI Publishing Studio

AI Publishing Studio Backup is a private utility that uses Google Drive to store encrypted backup snapshots of the owner's AI publishing development and operational records.

What it does

The utility uses Restic to encrypt backup archives before they are uploaded through rclone to a designated folder in the owner's Google Drive. It also lists snapshots, checks their integrity, and retrieves encrypted archives for restoration when needed.

This is not a public product, Google Drive file manager, or invitation for other people to connect their accounts. The OAuth application is used solely by its owner for backup and disaster recovery.

Why Google Drive access is requested

The utility needs Google Drive access to upload, find, verify, and restore encrypted backups in an existing Drive folder. It requests only the Google Drive drive.file permission, which limits the utility to files and folders that it created itself: the encrypted backup repository. It cannot see or open any other file in the owner's Drive.

Backup contents are encrypted on the owner's device before upload. Google Drive hosts the encrypted files but does not receive the Restic decryption passphrase through this website.

Privacy and contact

Read our privacy policy for what data is accessed, how it is used, stored, and deleted. For questions about this owner-operated application, use the support contact email shown on its Google OAuth consent screen.
